First assess and manage child's fluid needs  26. If child has vomiting with watery diarrhoea and has been in cholera outbreak area in past 5 days, cholera likely  64.
Then give urgent attention to the child with vomiting and any of:
• < 2 months old and vomiting (not refluxing)
• Vomits fresh or digested blood
• Unable to drink
• Continuous vomiting/vomiting everything
• Severe abdominal pain
• Headache
• No stool or wind for last 24 hours
• Several episodes of early morning vomiting
• Decreased level of consciousness  174
• Neck stiffness/bulging fontanelle
• Guarding and rigidity during peri-umbilical percussion, peritonitis likely
• Tender in right lower abdomen, appendicitis likely
• Painful groin swelling that will not reduce
• Distended abdomen
• Abdominal mass
• Jelly-like stool
• Bile-stained vomiting
Manage and refer urgently:
• Check fingerprick glucose. If ≤ 28 days old and glucose < 2.6mmol/L  9. If > 28 days old and glucose < 3mmol/L (< 4 if diabetes) or ≥ 11mmol/L  32.
• Give maintenance fluid IV according to reference guide  175
• Keep nil by mouth.
• If ≥ 2 of: temperature ≥ 38°C, headache, decreased level of consciousness  174, neck stiffness/bulging fontanelle, meningitis likely: give ceftriaxone1 100mg/kg IV/IM2  159:12.
• If baby < 2 months old and unable to feed: give ceftriaxone1 80mg/kg IM2  159:11 and keep warm.
• If appendicitis or peritonitis likely: give ceftriaxone1 80mg/kg IM2  159:11. If pain, give oral morphine  163:36.
