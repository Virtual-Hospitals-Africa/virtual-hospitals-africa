Give urgent attention to the child with jaundice and any of:
• Newborn within first 24 hours of birth
• Baby ≤ 10 days old with ≥ 10% weight loss
• Confused or decreased level of consciousness  174
• Hb < 8g/dL
• Easy bruising or bleeding
• Temperature ≥ 38°C
Manage and refer urgently:
• Check fingerprick glucose. If ≤ 28 days old and glucose < 2.6mmol/L  9. If > 28 days old and glucose < 3mmol/L (< 4 if diabetes) or ≥ 11mmol/L  32.
