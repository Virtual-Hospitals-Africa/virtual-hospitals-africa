Give urgent attention to the child with cough/difficulty breathing and any of:
• Baby < 2 months old
• History of apnoea (episodes of no breathing > 10 seconds)
• Unable to drink/feed
• Tires/sweats during feeds
• Lower chest indrawing
• Nasal flaring
• Grunting
• Stridor
• Blue lips/tongue
• Sats ≤ 92%
• Restless or irritable
• Lethargy or decreased level of consciousness  174
• Sudden onset of generalised itch/rash or face/tongue itch/swelling and any of: tight throat/
hoarseness, difficulty breathing, wheeze, shock1, dizziness/collapse, abdominal pain/nausea/
vomiting/diarrhoea or exposure to possible allergen2, anaphylaxis likely 37.
Manage and refer urgently:
• Give oxygen 2L/minute via nasal prongs. If < 1 year old with blocked nose, instil sodium chloride 0.9% solution 1 drop into each nostril and suction the nose.
• If wheeze 56 or if known heart problem 133.
• Check fingerprick glucose. If ≤ 28 days old and glucose < 2.6mmol/L  9. If > 28 days old and glucose < 3mmol/L (< 4 if diabetes) or ≥ 11mmol/L  32.
• Give ceftriaxone3 80mg/kg IM  159:11. Avoid injecting ≥ 1g IM at one injection site. If < 1 year old and CLHIV/HIV status unknown, also give single dose co-trimoxazole  160:18.
• If stridor, encourage carer to keep child calm:
 - Give prednisone 1-2mg/kg  164:41. Give 2mL adrenaline (1:1000) in 2mL sodium chloride 0.9% via nebuliser with oxygen at 8L/minute every 15 minutes until stridor disappears.
