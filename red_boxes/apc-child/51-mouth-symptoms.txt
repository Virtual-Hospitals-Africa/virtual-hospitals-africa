Give urgent attention to the child with mouth symptoms and any of:
• Unable to open mouth
• Sudden onset of generalised itch/rash or face/tongue itch/swelling and any of: tight throat/hoarseness, difficulty breathing, wheeze, shock1, dizziness/collapse,
abdominal pain/nausea/vomiting/diarrhoea or exposure to possible allergen2, anaphylaxis likely 37
Manage and refer urgently:
• Check fingerprick glucose. If ≤ 28 days old and glucose < 2.6mmol/L  9. If > 28 days old and glucose < 3mmol/L (< 4 if diabetes) or ≥ 11mmol/L  32.
