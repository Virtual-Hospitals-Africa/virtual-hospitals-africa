Give urgent attention to the child with tiredness or lethargy and any of:
• Unable to drink/breastfeed
• Baby < 3 months old
• Vomiting everything
• Decreased level of consciousness  174
• Increased respiratory rate  175 or breathing problem 55
• Bone pain or continuous pain
• Easy bruising or bleeding
• Likely meningitis:
 - If < 2 years old: bulging fontanelle (when not crying), refusing food/drink
 - Any age, ≥ 2 of: temperature ≥ 38°C, headache, decreased level of consciousness  174, neck stiffness
• Weight loss, poor weight gain/failure to thrive and pale palms/conjunctiva
Manage and refer urgently:
• Check fingerprick glucose  32 and fingerprick Hb  46.
• Assess and manage child’s fluid needs  26.
• If meningitis likely, give ceftriaxone1 100mg/kg IV/IM  159:12. Avoid injecting ≥ 1g IM at one injection site.
