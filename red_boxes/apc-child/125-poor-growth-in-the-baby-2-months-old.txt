Give urgent attention to the baby with poor growth and any of:
• Unable to feed
• Apnoea (episodes of no breathing > 10 seconds)
• Tires/sweats during feeds
• Lethargy or decreased level of consciousness  174
• Vomits everything
• Temperature < 35.5°C or ≥ 38°C
• Bulging fontanelle
• Reduced movements
• Difficulty breathing: respiratory rate > 60, grunting, nasal flaring or chest indrawing
• Diarrhoea (watery stools and/or > 3 stools/day)
• If ≤ 28 days old and glucose < 2.6mmol/L
• If > 28 days old and glucose < 3mmol/L (< 4 if diabetes)
Manage and refer urgently:
• If difficulty breathing, give oxygen at 1L/minute via nasal prongs.
• Assess and manage child's fluid needs  26.
• Check glucose, if ≤ 28 days old and < 2.6mmol/L  9. If > 28 days old and < 3mmol/L (< 4 if diabetes)  32. Otherwise, prevent hypoglycaemia: if alert, encourage breastfeeding or give
formula/F75®/sugar water2 3mL/kg/hour orally (use NGT if baby refusing or unable to feed). Feed at least 2 hourly until transfer.
• Treat for infection: give ceftriaxone3 80mg/kg IM  159:11. Avoid injecting ≥ 1g IM at one injection site.
• Keep baby warm: place baby skin to skin with mother or dress warmly including head and feet and cover with blanket.
