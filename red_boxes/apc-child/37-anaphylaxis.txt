Manage anaphylaxis and refer urgently:
• Give immediately adrenaline 0.01mg/kg (1:1000) IM into mid-outer thigh (see table). Repeat every 5 minutes if no response. If child has prescribed
auto-injector (like EpiPen®Junior 0.15mg or EpiPen® 0.3mg) available, use instead.
• Lie child flat, raise legs and give 100% face mask oxygen at 15L/min. Avoid lying child flat if difficulty breathing.
• If shock2 likely, give NaCl 0.9% 10mL/kg IV over 5-10 minutes. Repeat until signs of shock resolved. Stop if breathing worsens.
• If difficulty breathing or wheeze, nebulise with 2mL salbutamol 0.5% solution and 2mL ipratropium bromide 0.25mg/mL or give salbutamol 400-
600mcg (4-6 puffs) with metered dose inhaler (MDI) and large volume (500mL) spacer. Repeat every 5-10 minutes if no response. Assess and further
manage airway if needed  24.
• If generalised itch/rash or face/tongue swelling, give promethazine IM/slow IV 0.25mg/kg: if 2-5 years old (12-17.5kg), give 2.5mg. If 5-7 years old
(17.5-25kg), give 5mg. If 7-11 years old (25-35kg), give 7.5mg. If 11-15 years old (35-55kg), give 15mg  164:42. Avoid if < 2 years old or low BP  175.
