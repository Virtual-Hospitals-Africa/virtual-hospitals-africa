Give urgent attention to the child with diarrhoea and any of:
• Decreased level of consciousness  174
• Seizures  28
• Blood in stool in child < 1 year old
• Unable to drink
• Persistent vomiting
• Persistent temperature ≥ 38°C
• Shock1/severe dehydration2
• Guarding and rigidity during peri-umbilical percussion, peritonitis likely
• Swelling of legs/wasting, severe acute malnutrition (SAM) likely  126
• Persistent diarrhoea with dehydration despite treatment for 3 days
Manage and refer urgently:
• Check fingerprick glucose. If ≤ 28 days old and glucose < 2.6mmol/L  9. If > 28 days and glucose < 3 (< 4 if diabetes) or ≥ 11mmol/L  32. Also assess and manage child's fluids needs  26.
• If child < 1 year with blood in stool, or severe dehydration in baby < 28 days old/any child with SAM, give ceftriaxone2 100mg/kg IV/IM  159:12. Avoid injecting ≥ 1g IM at one injection site.
• Give vitamin A: if < 6 months old: 50 000IU; if 6-12 months old: 100 000IU; if ≥ 1 year old: 200 000IU.
• Give zinc: give 10mg (break scored 20mg tablet in half and give half a tablet).
