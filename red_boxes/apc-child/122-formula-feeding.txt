Give urgent attention to the formula feeding baby with any of:
• Unable to feed
• Vomits everything
• Chokes/coughs when feeding
• Difficulty swallowing (milk pools in mouth)
• Cleft palate
• Difficulty breathing, blue lips or sweats during feeds
• Baby lost ≥ 10% body weight
Manage and refer urgently:
• If baby < 2 months old and unable to feed: give ceftriaxone1 80mg/kg IM  159:11. Avoid injecting ≥ 1g IM at one injection site. Keep warm.
• Check glucose: If ≤ 28 days old and < 2.6mmol/L  9. If > 28 days old and < 3mmol/L (< 4 if diabetes)  32. Otherwise, prevent hypoglycaemia: give 3mL/kg of expressed breastmilk/
formula feed every hour via NGT.
