Give urgent attention to the child with back pain and any of:
• Child < 4 years old
• Significant TB exposure1  106 or current/previous TB
• Recent trauma or injury to back/spine 70
• Severe pain causing night waking/affecting daily activities
• Pain down leg
• Pins and needles in limb/s
• Temperature ≥ 38°C and/or weight loss
• Weakness in limb/s
• Sudden painful curve of spine
• New onset limp
• Urinary or bowel incontinence
