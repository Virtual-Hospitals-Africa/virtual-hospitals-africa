Give urgent attention to the child with pallor and/or a low Hb and any of:
• Hb < 7g/dL
• Baby < 6 months old
• Jaundice
• Swollen legs
• Increased respiratory rate  175
• Increased pulse rate  175
• Palpitations or chest pain
• Bone or joint pain
• Lethargy or decreased level of consciousness  174
• Purple/red rash that does not disappear with pressure
• Recent travel to a malaria area, malaria likely
• Weight loss, poor weight gain/failure to thrive
• Widespread/easy bruising or bleeding
Manage and refer urgently:
• If increased respiratory rate, give oxygen at 2L/minute via nasal prongs.
