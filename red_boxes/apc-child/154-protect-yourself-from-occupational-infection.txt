Give urgent attention to the health care worker who has had a sharps injury or splash to eye, mouth, nose or broken skin with exposure to any of:
• Blood
• Blood-stained fluid/tissue
• Pleural/pericardial/peritoneal/amniotic/synovial/cerebrospinal fluid
• Vaginal secretions
• Semen
• Breast milk
Management:
• If broken skin, clean area immediately with soap and water.
• If splash to eye, mouth or nose, immediately rinse mouth/nose or irrigate eye thoroughly with sodium chloride 0.9% or sterile water.
• Assess the need for HIV and hepatitis B post-exposure prophylaxis  PACK Adult.
