Give urgent attention to the child with burn/s and any of:
• Child < 1 year old
• Likely inhalation burn (burns to face/neck, hoarse, stridor or black sputum)
• Burn of face, hand, foot, genitals, joints
• Circumferential1 burn of chest/limbs
• Full-thickness burn
• Burn > 10% TBSA
• ≥ 2 of: cold hands/feet, weak/fast pulse  175, CRT2 ≥ 3 seconds,
decreased level of consciousness  174: shock likely  26
• Temperature ≥ 38°C
• Sudden skin swelling with redness, pain or warmth
• Electric/chemical burn
• Unable to drink/breastfeed
• Burn > 5% TBSA in child 1-2 years old
Manage and refer urgently to closest burns centre:
• Assess and manage airway, breathing and circulation  24.
• If burn > 10% TBSA, inhalational burn, drowsy/confused, give oxygen 2L/minute via nasal prongs. Doctor to consider intubation.
• Remove non-sticking clothing. Cool burn with cold running tap water or wet towel for 20 minutes. Avoid hypothermia.
• Give paracetamol 15mg/kg 6 hourly  163:37. If severe pain, give oral morphine according to age: if < 1 month give 0.05mg/kg 6 hourly, if 1-12 months give 0.1mg/kg 4-6 hourly, if ≥ 1
year give 0.2-0.4mg/kg 4-6 hourly  163:36. If oral morphine not available, give IV morphine 0.1mg/kg (up to 10mg) slowly over 3-5 minutes, 4 hourly as needed. If respiratory rate decreases
 175 or sats ≤ 92%, give oxygen 2L/minute via nasal prongs.
• Gently remove loose/dead skin, clean with chlorhexidine 0.05%. Re-assess to confirm %TBSA burnt  39.
• Give IV fluids:
 - If shock likely, manage child's fluid needs  26.
 - If > 10% TBSA: give NaCl 0.9% IV 4mL x weight (kg) x %TBSA over first 24 hours. Give half this volume in first 8 hours from time of burn.
 - In addition, give maintenance fluids: ORS orally/via NGT or NaCl 0.9% with dextrose 5%3 IV, according to weight  175.
 - If other injuries, manage  33.
•  Dress the burn:
 - If first aid burns dressing (e.g. Burnshield®) available: apply for 4 hours. If transfer delayed > 4 hours/or first aid burn dressing not available, use paraffin guaze dressing instead.
 - If full thickness or > 10% TBSA burn, apply paraffin gauze and cover with plastic wrap.
 - If hospital transfer delayed > 12 hours, apply paraffin gauze then cover with dry gauze and bandage.
• Keep child warm: cover with a clean dry sheet and blanket to prevent hypothermia. Monitor temperature.
• Give tetanus toxoid 0.5mL IM if not had in last 5 years (check RtHB).
• Reassess airway, breathing and circulation hourly  24.
