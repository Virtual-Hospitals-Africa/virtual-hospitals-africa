Give urgent attention to the child with a lump/swelling in neck, axilla or groin and any of:
• Firm painful swelling in groin area, vomiting or not passing stool, incarcerated/strangulated inguinal hernia likely
• Swelling in neck with wheeze or difficulty breathing
• Red overlying skin, painful to touch and soft (fluctuant) in middle of swelling, abscess likely
Refer urgently.
