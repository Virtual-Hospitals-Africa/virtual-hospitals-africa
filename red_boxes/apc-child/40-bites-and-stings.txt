Give urgent attention to the child with bite/sting and any of:
• Snakebite1 (even if bite not seen) or venom in eyes
• Scorpion1 sting
• Deep or large wound needing sutures
• Actively bleeding
• Weakness
• Drooping eyelids
• Difficulty swallowing/speaking
• Double vision
• Spider1 bite with drooling, restlessness, muscle cramps, erection
• Sudden onset of generalised itch/rash or face/tongue itch/swelling and any of: tight throat/
hoarseness, difficulty breathing, wheeze, shock2, dizziness/collapse, abdominal pain/nausea/
vomiting/diarrhoea or exposure to possible allergen3, anaphylaxis likely 37
•  Bite involves hand
Manage and refer urgently:
• If bleeding, apply direct pressure to stop bleeding. Assess and manage child’s fluid needs  26.
• Wash wound well with chlorhexidine 0.05% or povidone iodine 10% solution and irrigate under running water for 10-15 minutes then apply dressing. Do not suture.
• If snake/spider bite or scorpion sting: avoid applying tourniquet, squeezing/cutting the bite or sucking out venom. Contact Poisons Information Helpline  170.
• If venom in eyes: instil 1 drop oxybuprocaine 0.4% eye drops then flush under running water or sodium chloride 0.9% for 20 minutes. Avoid giving oxybuprocaine to take home.
• If pain, give paracetamol 15mg/kg  163:37. If very painful scorpion sting, doctor to inject lidocaine 2% 2mL around site.
• Give tetanus toxoid 0.5mL IM if not had in last 5 years (check RtHB).
