Manage and refer urgently:
• Assess and manage airway, breathing and circulation  24. If difficulty breathing, give oxygen 2L/min via nasal prongs.
• If poison on skin: health worker to wear gloves, apron and mask. Remove clothes and wash skin with soap and water.
• If poison in eye/s: immediately flush under running water or sodium chloride 0.9% for at least 20 minutes.
• Ask carers about time of exposure, amount and type of substance: examine tablets, packets, containers of suspected substance. Determine if toxic dose ingested:
 - If toxic dose swallowed within past hour and child fully conscious, give activated charcoal 1g/kg with 50-100mL water  157:3. Avoid if child swallowed petrol/paraffin/iron/lithium/
alcohol/corrosives (e.g. household detergents). Avoid inducing vomiting/doing gastric lavage unless specifically instructed by Poison Helpline due to aspiration risk.
• If pinpoint pupils, excessive drooling/sweating, coughing up or choking on secretions, vomiting/diarrhoea, slow pulse  175, organophosphate poisoning likely: give atropine 0.05mg/kg IV  158:9.
If no response, double the dose every 3-5 minutes until breathing and sats improve and heart rate normal  175 (pupils will take longer to improve). Monitor closely and give further doses if needed.
• If shallow breathing/decreased respiratory rate  175 and opioid3 overdose suspected: give 100% oxygen via bag-valve mask at 10-15L/min and naloxone 0.1 mg/kg IV/IM (up to 2mg)
immediately. Reassess every 2 minutes: if respiratory rate still decreased, give additional dose every 2 minutes up to a total dose of 10mg. Monitor closely and give further doses if needed.
• Monitor for recurrence of respiratory depression. If respiratory depression recurs discuss further management with specialist.
• If dystonic reaction: give biperiden 0.1mg/kg IM/slow IV (up to 2mg if < 7 years; 3mg if 7-10 years; 5mg if ≥ 10 years) or promethazine 0.5mg/kg IM (up to 12.5mg if < 10 years; 25mg if ≥ 10 years).
• Notify2 if child with symptoms has been exposed to pesticides (like organophosphates, rat poison), mercury/lead.
