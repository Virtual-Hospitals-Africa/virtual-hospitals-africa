Give urgent attention to the child with known heart problem and any of:
• < 1 year old and difficulty feeding or sweats during feeds: heart failure likely
• Any child with known heart problem and difficulty breathing/tires easily/new onset increased pulse rate  175, heart failure likely
• Fainting
• Sudden weakness of one side of body
• Blue skin/lips
• Temperature ≥ 38°C or fever in last 3 days
• Chest pain
Manage and refer urgently:
• If heart failure likely, give furosemide 1mg/kg IV over 5 minutes  161:25. Avoid IV fluids.
• Assess and manage child’s fluid needs  26.
• If difficulty breathing/increased respiratory rate  175 and temperature ≥ 38°C/fever in last few days: give ceftriaxone1 80mg/kg IM  159:11. Avoid injecting ≥ 1g IM at one injection site.
• Give oxygen at 2L/minute via nasal prongs and raise head of bed to 45 degrees.
